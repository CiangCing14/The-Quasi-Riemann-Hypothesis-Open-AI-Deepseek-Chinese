The proof will construct one character sum with two exact representations. Cubic-theta reflection

4     Arithmetic and analytic preliminaries
The two parts use the same arithmetic conventions and analytic estimates. This section fixes the
residue symbols, retaining their zero values even for principal powers, and proves the Gauss and
reciprocity identities used to transform character sums. It then establishes a calculus for smooth
norm profiles and uniform bounds for a Hecke L-function on a disk known to be zero-free. These
are the common preliminaries for the balanced argument.
