6     The base probe and its balanced low estimate
We now place the completed sums of the preceding section inside one average Iη (X, Y, Z). Its
original representation separates into an additive polynomial and a completed theta row. At the
balanced scales X = Y = Z 1/2 , their mean-square estimates give the direct bound Iη ≪η Z 1/4+ϵ .
We first define the average for independent positive scales X, Y, Z; only the final proposition in
this section specializes them. The next section applies Poisson summation to this same average
and identifies the reciprocal of the target L-function in its principal row.

6.1   Definition and separation of the two factors
Choose a finite ray group T , independently of the target character, through which the functions
R, G, and the fixed primary and supplementary phases supported over 2, 3 in Lemma 4.4 factor.
The target-dependent fixed-numerator characters below need not factor through T . For a finite-
order Hecke character η, write

Use one excluded set S for the finite family {ηθ : θ ∈ Tb}. It contains the primes over 6, the prime
supports of the defining moduli of the fixed zero-extended presentations of η and the characters

arithmetic data. They are fixed before X, Y, Z and the varying row and prime parameters are
chosen. Enlarging S will not change T .

these two properties.
