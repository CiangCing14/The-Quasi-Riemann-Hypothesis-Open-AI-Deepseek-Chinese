    Logarithmic Fourier separation uses a joint cutoff in a coordinate list containing every nor-

preselected small power. The cutoff and its full support depend only on the block centers and
fixed data, not on the individual values of the labels inside a row norm. Provided the coupled

Fourier density is common to those rows, as required by Lemma 4.5; separate uniform bounds for
row-dependent densities would not imply the same Hilbert-space inequality. Nonsmooth masks
are retained or resolved by exact arithmetic identities, never differentiated or incorporated as
label-dependent sharp Fourier selectors.
